Parent setup: connect the Khan routine

Use the existing Khan student accounts and add each child to a Khan teacher class. Grade 4: 4th grade math, 4th grade reading and vocab, plus targeted Grammar. Grade 7: 7th grade math, 7th grade reading/grammar/vocab, selected middle-school science units, and early World History selections.

For each week below, assign the named lessons in Khan in order, with Friday quiz/exercise checks and module-end completed-unit tests. Long-passage and history selections may need exercise or written checks rather than native unit tests. The website’s course links open the named courses; they do not create Khan assignments or sync scores.

Print the daily packet for gap work. Use Education.com worksheets only when practice or gap coverage is needed. Record first-attempt assessment scores before retakes, and carry unfinished units forward rather than testing untaught content.
