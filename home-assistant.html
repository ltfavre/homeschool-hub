THE KIDS' SCHOOL SCREEN

The kids work from the family dashboard. You teach from here.

Home Assistant shows each child's lessons for the day, the assignments you create here, what to read now, and documents you share with their grade. When they check something off on the tablet, it's marked done or turned in here.

What the kids see

  • Today's lessons: one checkbox per subject. Checking it marks the lesson done in the gradebook's daily-work list.
  • Assignments: everything you publish on the Assignments page. Checking one off turns it in (it shows as “Turned in” for you to grade). Graded work shows the score and your feedback and can't be unchecked.
  • Reading now: this module's book with a read-online link and discussion prompts.
  • From your teacher: Library documents shared with their grade, and files attached to open assignments.

File links on the tablet are signed and expire after one to two weeks; they refresh on their own. Private documents and other students' work are never shown.

School connection key

Home Assistant uses this key instead of a password. It can read school agendas, check off lessons and assignments, and open shared files. It can't enter grades, change accounts or passwords, or open answer keys.
