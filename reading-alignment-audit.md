# Reading alignment audit (Grades 4 and 7) — 2026-10-21

Method: for every math, ELA, science and social studies week, compared the week's topic with each assigned reading's section title and book unit, then reviewed every low-match week by hand. 288 subject-weeks checked. Page numbers and chapter text were not re-verified.

## Well aligned
- Math, both grades: readings match topics (every lesson title fits).
- Grade 7 social studies: chapters match the history topics in order.
- ELA, both grades: topics are skills (context clues, evidence) applied to the novel chapters, so titles do not need to match. Spanish workbook units are mixed into ELA by design.

## Grade-level concerns (Grade 7 science)
Modules 5-6 (weeks 5-1 to 6-4) use elementary-level readers: Grade 3 review (Forces), Grade 4 units (Energy, Waves; same readers as Grade 4) and a Grade 5 astronomy reader. Grade 7 social studies also leans on readers adapted from publisher grade 5 or 6 for Greece/Rome and the Reformation to Enlightenment.

## Not fixed (needs new resources)
Grade 7: nervous/skeletal/muscular systems, levers and mechanical advantage. Grade 4: electricity and conductors, simple machines, disease, Mississippi state and local government, region-specific Northeast/Midwest/West facts.
