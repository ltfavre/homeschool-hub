Each course has 36 short chapters with a reading, guided activity, application, and check. Chapter N is PDF page N + 1. Parent guides include explanations, expected answers, and practical scoring. These are introductory teaching booklets, supplemented by publisher books and activities.

Weekly connections and readings

Teaching approach

Health, puberty, and reproduction are factual and age appropriate. Discuss hypothetical situations and allow questions without requesting private disclosures. Religion and philosophy are studied through descriptions, evidence, comparison, and critical thinking; assess understanding and reasoning rather than belief. Supervise tools, cooking, and first-aid practice.

ELA includes sustained reading, literature discussion, vocabulary, spelling patterns, grammar/editing, writing, speaking/listening, and beginner Spanish. Give English reading and writing the main ELA block, then use a short supported Spanish segment on both Tuesday and Thursday.

The downloaded binder library contains publisher PDFs and separate student/teacher folders. Some supporting documents combine instructor directions with reproducible student pages; a parent selects the student pages. The grades 6–8 health lesson guide is a selection guide, not a full health textbook.
